# Replacing Starter Content

Some documentation pages begin with a **Starter content** callout. These callouts are intentionally visible on the deployed site so your team, instructor, and graders can quickly see which assignment pages still need project-specific work.

Remove a starter-content callout only after the page underneath it has been replaced with real content for your project.

## What to Replace

Replace starter outlines, placeholder tables, placeholder OpenAPI files, and generic section prompts with information from your own project.

## When to Keep It

Keep a starter-content callout on future milestone pages until that assignment is complete. The site can still build and deploy with future milestone pages unfinished.

## What Not to Do

Do not remove the callout just to make the warning disappear. A page is ready when the content is specific enough that a reader can understand and evaluate your team's actual system.
